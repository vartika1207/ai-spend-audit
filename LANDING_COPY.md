## FAQ
**Is this free?** Yes, the audit is free and requires no login.

**Do I need to enter my email first?** No. The result is shown before email capture.

**How are savings calculated?** The audit uses deterministic rules based on team size, plan fit, use case, and current spend.

**Does AI decide the recommendation?** No. AI only writes the personalized summary paragraph. The math is hardcoded and tested.

**What happens for high-savings companies?** If savings are above $500/month, the report recommends exploring Credex discounted AI credits.
